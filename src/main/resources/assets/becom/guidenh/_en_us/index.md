# Better Communications

Better Communications adds telephones, radios, mobile phones and satellites to GregTech. Every call and every radio
channel is real voice, carried by GTNH Voice. How well you hear someone depends on the path between you: how far it
runs, the hills in the way, how deep underground each end stands, and the weather.

The quest book's **Better Communications** tab takes you through the machines in order. This guide holds the details.
Point at a BeCom item or quest and hold <KeyBind id="key.guidenh.open_guide" /> to open its page.

## The numbers behind a link

Every link in BeCom ends at one of five levels, from None to Max. [How signal works](signal.md) shows where the level
comes from, and [the Signal Analyzer](analyzer.md) shows it for wherever you stand.

## Machines in the rain

GTNH keeps GregTech's rain hazards on, and they reach BeCom's machines as they reach any GT machine that stores
power: one left in the rain can catch fire or explode. That's the Line Terminal, the Repeater and Router Heads, the
Transceiver, the Videophone, the ME Bridge and the Portal Relay, and the energy hatches of antennas, the Satellite
Launch Pad and the Satellite Dish. Plain Pylon Heads, controllers and other hatches are safe.

A machine's top is in the rain when nothing stands above it, and a side when nothing stands beside it at its height or
higher. A cover on that face keeps the rain off. The side a cable comes in by needs none: the cable shields it. Covers
don't count against the open sky the pad and the dish need.
